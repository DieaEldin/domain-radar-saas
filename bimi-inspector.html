What is BIMI?

BIMI is an email specification that allows domain owners to display official brand logos next to authenticated emails in supported inboxes (Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail).

SVG Tiny PS Required

BIMI logos must strictly follow the SVG Tiny Portable/Secure format. Standard SVG/PNG images are rejected by secure email providers.

DMARC Enforcement

BIMI requires a strict DMARC policy (p=quarantine or p=reject). Domains with p=none cannot render BIMI logos.
