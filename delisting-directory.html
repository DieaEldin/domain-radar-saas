IP & Domain Blacklist Delisting Directory

Direct official portal links and step-by-step instructions to request removal for your mail server or domain from major global security blocklists.

Spamhaus Removal

Covers SBL, XBL, CSS, and PBL lists. Highest impact on global email delivery.

  1. Fix open relays or compromised email accounts.
  2. Verify valid Reverse DNS (PTR) and SPF/DMARC records.
  3. Search your IP/Domain on Spamhaus IP Checker.
  4. Submit the step-by-step delist request form.
Request Spamhaus Delist →

Barracuda Central

Widely used by enterprise email gateways and corporate security filters.

  1. Ensure outbound spam traffic has completely stopped.
  2. Confirm your PTR record matches your HELO/EHLO hostname.
  3. Fill out the Barracuda IP Removal Request form.
Request Barracuda Delist →

SpamCop Blocking List

Automatically lists IPs based on spam traps and real-time user reports.

  1. Identify and halt the source of automated spam.
  2. SpamCop listings automatically expire within 24–48 hours after spam ceases.
  3. Check status directly on SpamCop lookup.
Check SpamCop Status →

Microsoft Outlook / SmartScreen

Affects delivery to @outlook.com, @hotmail.com, and Office 365 inboxes.

  1. Join the SNDS (SmartNetwork Data Services) program.
  2. Ensure strict DKIM and DMARC alignment.
  3. Submit an official Sender Information Support request.
Microsoft Delist Portal →

Google Postmaster Tools

Maintains domain and IP reputation for delivery to all @gmail.com accounts.

  1. Add your domain to Google Postmaster Tools.
  2. Maintain spam rate strictly below 0.1% (never exceed 0.3%).
  3. Ensure 100% SPF & DKIM authentication pass rate.
Google Postmaster →

SORBS (Proofpoint)

Monitors spam traps, open relays, and dynamic IP space allocations.

  1. Create a free account on the SORBS Support System.
  2. Locate your listed IP address in their database.
  3. Open a delisting ticket specifying the resolution implemented.
SORBS Lookup & Delist →

UCEPROTECT Network

Monitors spam traps across Level 1 (Single IP), Level 2, and Level 3 (Subnets/ASNs).

  1. Verify individual IP listing vs. entire subnet ban.
  2. Stop all spam or misconfigured mailers on your network.
  3. Listings automatically expire after 7 consecutive spam-free days.
UCEPROTECT Lookup →

Backscatterer (IPSPS)

Focuses on misconfigured servers sending misdirected bounce messages (backscatter).

  1. Disable non-standard out-of-office automated replies.
  2. Ensure NDRs (Non-Delivery Reports) are not sent to forged senders.
  3. Auto-delists after 4 weeks without new violations, or use manual option.

Real-time reporting service monitoring SSH, Mail, FTP, and web attacks/spam.

  1. Secure your server against brute-force and unauthorized access.
  2. Search your IP on their public lookup interface.
  3. Submit an unblock request directly via their delist form.

  1. Monitor bounce rates and keep spam complaints below 0.2%.

  3. Review reputation report and request blocklist remediation if needed.
